Preservation Protocol

Healing is 50% of the Art

Your body art is an investment. Strictly adhere to our clinical aftercare regimen during the primary 14-day healing cycle to preserve pigmentation crispness.

Essential Rituals (Do's)

  • • Remove protective second-skin dressing gently under warm running water after 3 to 4 hours.
  • • Clean exclusively with fragrance-free, antibacterial soap using clean bare hands.
  • • Apply a razor-thin layer of authorized tattoo balm 3 times daily. Keep it hydrated, never soggy.
  • • Wear loose, breathable 100% cotton garments to minimize mechanical friction against fresh ink.

Strict Restrictions (Don'ts)

  • • Never submerge your tattoo in standing bodies of water (pools, tubs, ocean) for 3 weeks minimum.
  • • Do not pick, scratch, or peel skin flakes. Premature flaking extracts the pigment underneath.
  • • Avoid direct sun exposure and tanning beds during the first month. Use SPF 50+ only once fully healed.
  • • Do not re-bandage the tattoo after the initial wrap is discarded. The skin requires oxygen to heal.
